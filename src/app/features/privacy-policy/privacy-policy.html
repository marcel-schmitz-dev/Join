3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. When you visit the site, the web server automatically processes technical data that your browser transmits: your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested page or file, the amount of data transferred, the HTTP status code, and browser and operating system information (user agent).

This processing is necessary to deliver the website, to ensure its stability and security, and to detect and defend against abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functional website). The log data is stored only for as long as required for these purposes. IONOS processes this data on my behalf. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR has been concluded with IONOS.

4. Registration, login and guest access

For authentication and data storage, Join uses the backend service Supabase (see section 6). You can use the application in two ways:

  • Registration: When you sign up, the name, email address and password you enter are processed. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. In addition, Supabase records technical metadata of your account and sessions, such as creation time, last login and IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (providing the requested user account and the functions of the application).
  • Guest login: A guest session does not require a name or email address. Supabase creates an anonymous account with a randomly generated ID so that the application can be tested. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR.

5. Contacts, tasks and other content you enter

The application stores the content you create, in particular contacts (name, email address, phone number, avatar colour) and tasks (title, description, due date, priority, category, subtasks, assigned contacts, status). This data is stored in the Supabase database in order to display and synchronise it within the application. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Visibility of contacts: Access to contacts is restricted in the database:

  • When you register, a contact with your name and email address is created automatically. This contact is visible to other registered users of the application. Guests cannot see it.
  • Registered users can see this profile contact, the sample contacts and the contacts they created themselves. They can only edit or delete their own profile contact and the contacts they created themselves.
  • Guests can only see, edit and delete the shared sample contacts (dummy data) and the contacts they created themselves during their guest session.

Please note the following:

  • Tasks and their content are shared between all logged-in users of the application, including guests. Do not enter confidential information in tasks.
  • The application is a demonstration and portfolio project. Please use fictitious data for contacts and tasks, and do not enter personal data of third parties or any sensitive information (such as health data) in the application.

7. Local storage in your browser (no tracking cookies)

Join does not use tracking or advertising cookies and does not use analytics or marketing tools. The application uses the storage of your browser (local storage and session storage) only for functions that are technically necessary or that you have requested:

  • the login session token of Supabase, so that you stay logged in while using the application (local storage),
  • your chosen display theme, so that it is remembered on your next visit (local storage),
  • a flag that the login animation has already been shown during the current visit (session storage).

Access to this information is permitted without consent under § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, as it is strictly necessary to provide the service you have requested or to store your own settings. Any subsequent processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings; you will then be logged out.

8. Fonts and external resources

The fonts used in the application are hosted locally on the same server. No fonts or scripts are loaded from external servers, so your IP address is not transmitted to font providers or content delivery networks when you visit the site.

9. Storage period and deletion

Personal data is stored only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above. You can delete your account at any time in the application: open your own profile in Contacts and choose Delete. This removes your account and your contact entry. Data created in guest mode is not linked to a name or email address and may be deleted periodically without notice. Server log files are deleted after a short period in accordance with the settings of the hosting provider.

If you would like me to delete your data or you cannot delete it yourself, please contact me using the details in section 2.

10. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw a given consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

To exercise your rights, simply send me a message using the contact details in section 2.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

11. Obligation to provide data, automated decisions

Using Join with an account requires a name, an email address and a password; without them, registration is not possible. The guest login works without personal details. There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

12. Data security

The website and the connection to the database use SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS), so that data transmitted between your browser and the servers cannot be read by third parties. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. No system is completely secure, so I recommend not using a password that you use elsewhere.

13. Changes to this privacy policy

I will update this privacy policy when the application or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available on this page.
